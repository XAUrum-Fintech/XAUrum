Privacy Policy

Xaurum Fintech Private Limited · Version 2026-09-27-crash · Effective 27 September 2026

Xaurum Fintech Private Limited may process your mobile number, OTP and account-security records, name, email, city, profile details, watchlists, alerts, device information, logs, support requests, voice notes you choose to send, and dealer-link activity.

We use this information to authenticate accounts, provide watchlists and alerts, improve reliability, prevent misuse, respond to requests, send service messages, and comply with law.

We do not share your mobile number with a dealer merely because you register or view a listing. If you intentionally use a dealer handoff or submit information to a dealer, that dealer's privacy policy applies.

Service providers may process information for hosting, databases, authentication, OTP or WhatsApp delivery, notifications, security, analytics, support, and backups.

To fix app problems, orob sends crash reports with stack traces and device state to Google Firebase Crashlytics. Reports use only a Firebase install id, not an orob account id. Crash diagnostics do not include your phone number, name, email, text you type, precise location, or calculator inputs in custom fields. We do not use this data for advertising or share it with advertisers. Crashlytics retention and deletion follow its service controls.

You may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of optional communications by contacting contact@xaurum.in. orob is not intended for children under 18.
