By email

If you can't access the app, email support@xaurum.in from the address or number on your account and ask us to delete your account. Please include:

  • The mobile number registered on your account
  • Your name (as entered in the app, if any)

We'll confirm once your account and associated data have been deleted, usually within a few business days.

What gets deleted

Your profile, watchlists, alerts preferences, and account-security records. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business records, such as support correspondence.
